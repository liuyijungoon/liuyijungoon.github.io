Yijun Liu

PhD Candidate in Computer Science

Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen  ·  Shenzhen, China

I am a fourth-year PhD candidate at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen (HIT-SZ), advised by Prof. Jingyong Su. My research centers on hallucination detection and uncertainty estimation in multimodal large language models (MLLMs), and failure detection under real-world distribution shifts. I have published at top-tier venues including NeurIPS, AAAI, IEEE TCSVT, and ICASSP, and serve as a reviewer for ICLR, NeurIPS, and CVPR.
